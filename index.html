When does an epigenetic edit become a pharmacology result?

A small notebook for connecting AAV delivery, locus remethylation, DUX4 suppression, and muscle-cell rescue.

Built by Cathy Liu for the Research Associate II / Senior Research Associate, In Vitro Pharmacology position

Evidence chain Study design Assay timing Public data

Epicrispr’s EPI-321 program asks more than whether an editor reaches a cell. The full claim depends on a linked series of observations: AAV delivery, expression of the compact dCas cargo, remethylation at D4Z4, suppression of DUX4 and its downstream genes, lower apoptosis, and recovery of engineered-muscle function.

I built this notebook to keep that causal chain visible. It separates measured public findings from adjustable planning thresholds, then shows which experiment should come next when delivery, mechanism, transcript, and phenotype disagree.

Design interpretation

Six donors preserve the donor breadth in Epicrispr’s detailed public in-vitro panel. Two controls can hold vehicle and a non-targeting editor while four doses estimate potency.

03

Put each readout where it can explain the next one

A later phenotype is hard to interpret without earlier delivery and mechanism measurements. Select a failure to see the smallest useful follow-up.

If delivery is weak

Repeat vector-genome dPCR alongside dCasONYX RT-qPCR. If both are low, optimize AAV exposure or cell state before interpreting DUX4.

The practical inference

The strongest candidate is not simply the one with the lowest DUX4 value. It is the one whose delivery, chromatin change, transcript response, cell survival, and muscle function remain consistent across donors and time.

Why this fits the position

The role asks for disease-relevant mammalian, primary-cell, and iPSC-derived assays; qPCR, RT-qPCR, dPCR, Western blot, ELISA, flow, imaging, and NGS; and experiments that establish potency, durability, specificity, and mechanism. This notebook turns those responsibilities into one interpretable experiment rather than a list of disconnected readouts.

What I would bring

I would bring that combination to careful assay execution, fast troubleshooting, and clear interpretation of multi-readout pharmacology studies.

Methods, limits, and sources

Measured values are transcribed from Epicrispr’s public 2024 WMS and ESGCT EPI-321 posters. The default evidence preset uses public cohort summaries, not raw individual-level data. All pass thresholds, alternative failure scenarios, plate calculations, and recommended follow-ups are illustrative planning aids rather than Epicrispr data or SOPs.
