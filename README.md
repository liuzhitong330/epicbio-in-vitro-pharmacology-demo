# Epicrispr in-vitro pharmacology demo

A role-specific interactive research note built by Cathy Liu for Epicrispr Biotechnologies' Research Associate II / Senior Research Associate, In Vitro Pharmacology position.

The demo connects the role's central responsibilities:

- design of disease-relevant primary-cell pharmacology studies;
- orthogonal dPCR, RT-qPCR, targeted methylation, imaging, and muscle-function readouts;
- interpretation of potency, mechanism, durability, and phenotypic rescue.

Its evidence backbone is Epicrispr's public 2024 EPI-321 poster data in patient-derived FSHD myoblasts and engineered muscle tissue. Public measurements are separated from illustrative planning thresholds and failure scenarios. No proprietary data or company SOPs are used.
